How it works

A smoother upper face

The temples are one of the first places to lose volume, often in people who are slim or active. Hollow temples can make the brow look heavy and the upper face look narrow and tired. A small amount of filler restores a soft, even curve from the forehead to the cheekbone.

Fills hollows

Restores the soft curve at the side of the brow.

Supports the brow

A subtle lift to the outer brow.

Smooths the outline

An even frame from forehead to cheekbone.

Looks rested

Less of the tired, hollow look.

Hyaluronic acid fillers are FDA-approved, and some are approved specifically for temple hollows. We’ll tell you which product we recommend for you.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • Hollow or sunken temples
  • A narrow or tired-looking upper face
  • Volume loss from weight loss or exercise
  • A subtle, natural refresh

Does not treat

  • Forehead lines
  • A heavy brow from loose skin
  • Pigmentation or texture

Pairs wellFor forehead lines, see Botox® & Xeomin®. For a gradual, full-face refresh, see Sculptra®.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    Material
    Cross-linked hyaluronic acid (HA). HA is a sugar your body already makes, and it holds water to keep skin smooth and plump. Cross-linking turns it into a stable gel that stays in place.
  2. 02
    Why it suits the temples
    Temple hollows need smooth, even volume across a wide area. We use an HA with enough lift to restore the natural curve, placed deep so the surface stays smooth.
  3. 03
    Comfort
    Most HA fillers are made with lidocaine, a local anesthetic, mixed into the gel.
  4. 04
    Reversible
    If you ever want to adjust or remove it, HA can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase.
  5. 05
    Over time
    Your body breaks HA down gradually and naturally, so results soften over time rather than ending suddenly.
  6. 06
    Our approach
    1. The full frame. We look at the temples together with the brow, forehead and cheekbones.
    2. Small amounts. We place filler conservatively and build gradually, for a smooth, natural curve.
    3. Long-lasting options. Sculptra® can be used here to restore volume gradually over time.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

We apply a numbing cream first, and most fillers also contain a local anesthetic. Most clients feel only light pressure.

Will I look different?

Most people simply look more rested. Temple filler restores a natural curve rather than adding obvious volume.

What are the side effects?

Swelling, bruising, redness or tenderness are common and usually settle within a few days to a week. Less often: small lumps or unevenness, which can usually be corrected. Rarely, filler can block a blood vessel. This needs immediate care, so call us right away if you notice unusual pain, skin color changes or any change in your vision.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, if you have an active skin infection or cold sore in the area, or if you have a history of severe allergies to filler ingredients. Talk to us first about bleeding disorders, autoimmune conditions or recent procedures in the area.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. It is often planned with Under-Eye Hollows, Botox® & Xeomin® or Sculptra®.

How should I prepare?

For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless they are prescribed for you. Plan at least 2 weeks before a big event. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

Mild swelling, tenderness or bruising is normal for a few days. Use a cold compress as needed. Skip intense workouts, saunas and alcohol for 24–48 hours. Don’t press or massage the area unless we ask you to. You may feel mild tenderness when chewing for a few days.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Visible right away, settling in about 2 weeks. How long it lasts depends on the product and your body. Results vary by individual. We see you for a check-in at about 2 weeks, then maintenance as needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
