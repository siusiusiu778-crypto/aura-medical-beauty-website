How it works

Smoother skin, from within

Gold RF Microneedling combines two treatments in one. Fine, gold-plated microneedles create tiny channels in the skin to start its natural repair process, while radiofrequency energy gently heats the deeper layers to tighten and rebuild collagen and elastin. Depth and energy are adjusted for each area, so results look natural and recovery is short.

Two ways to rebuild collagen, in one treatment.

Refines pores

Visibly smaller, tighter-looking pores.

Softens acne scars

Smooths the depth and edges of acne scars.

Smooths texture

Softer fine lines and a smoother surface.

Evens tone

Fades post-acne marks and dullness.

Numbing cream is applied first, and every treatment uses a sterile, single-use tip.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream first. Most clients feel warmth and light pressure.

How much downtime is there?

Expect redness, like a mild sunburn, that improves within a day and fades over 3–7 days. Most clients go back to their normal routine the next day, but skip makeup for the first few days.

What are the side effects?

Redness, mild swelling and warmth are common. Around the eyes and cheekbones, light bruising can appear and fades in about a week. Some clients see a few small breakouts. Infection and pigment changes are rare.

Is it safe for all skin tones?

Yes. Because the energy goes beneath the surface, it is suitable for most skin tones, including deeper skin.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy, with active breakouts, infection or cold sores in the area, within 6 months of isotretinoin, or if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device. Tell us about any history of keloid scarring.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. It pairs well with Sculptra® for volume and stretch marks, and a Barrier Recovery Facial helps your skin recover. Between sessions, a HydraFacial® keeps pores clear.

How should I prepare?

Pause retinol and exfoliating acids for a few days. Avoid sunburn in the area. If you’ve taken isotretinoin, please wait 6 months after your last dose. Tell us if you get cold sores. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

Redness and mild swelling are normal. Most improve within 24 hours and fade in 3–7 days. Keep the area dry for 8 hours, then rinse with cool water. For 3 days, cleanse with water only, moisturize gently and skip makeup. For 7 days, skip intense workouts, saunas and hot baths, and protect your skin from the sun.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Skin can look smoother within days. Collagen keeps building over the following months. A full series gives the most visible results. Results vary by individual. Most clients do a series of 3 treatments, spaced to let your skin rebuild in between. Then once a year to maintain.
