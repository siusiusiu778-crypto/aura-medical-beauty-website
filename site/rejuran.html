How we design it

Layered for your skin

Your Gold Skin Analysis tells us where your skin is dry, dull or sensitive.

  1. Analyze We check hydration, tone and sensitivity first.
  2. Plan We choose the areas and depth based on your analysis.
  3. Map Even droplets across the face, neck or hands.
  4. Repeat A series of 3 builds results that last.

Recommended for

Is this right for you?

  • Dull, tired-looking skin
  • Dry or dehydrated skin, especially in winter
  • Before a wedding or big event
  • Fine lines that come from dryness
  • Anyone who wants glow without volume

What to expect

Your timeline

Day 0

Numbing cream, then 30 minutes of treatment. Small bumps are normal.

Days 1–2

Bumps settle. Makeup the next day.

Weeks 1–2

Skin looks plumper and more radiant.

Session 2–3

Results build and last longer with each session.

Aftercare

  • Small bumps settle within 1–2 days.
  • No makeup until the next morning.
  • Skip saunas, hot yoga and alcohol for 24 hours.
  • Use gentle skincare and SPF for 3 days.

Please wait if you…

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have active acne, cold sores or infection on the face.
  • Have had filler or laser in the same area in the last 2 weeks.

FAQs

Good questions

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream first. Most clients feel light pressure.

Will it change my face shape?

No. Rejuran® adds hydration and glow, not volume.

How long does it last?

After a series of 3, most clients maintain with one session every 2–3 months.

Can I wear makeup?

Wait until the next day.

Is it included in membership?

AURA Prime members receive a BBL™ or Rejuran® session every 3 months.

How much does it cost?

Every plan is different. The number of units, sessions and the right combination are decided with your provider at a complimentary consultation, and you’ll receive a clear quote before any treatment.
