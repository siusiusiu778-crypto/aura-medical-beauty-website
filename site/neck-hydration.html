How it works

The neck, cared for

The skin on the neck is thinner than on the face, with fewer oil glands, so it dries out and loses firmness sooner. Rejuran® is made of polynucleotides (PN), purified DNA fragments derived from salmon. Placed just beneath the surface of the neck in tiny, evenly spaced drops, it supports the skin’s own repair process, helping it hold more moisture and become stronger, smoother and more elastic over time.

Is it right for you?

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream first. Most clients feel quick, light pricks across the area.

Will I have marks afterward?

Small bumps where the drops were placed are normal and usually settle within a day or two. Mild redness or a little bruising can also appear and fades within days.

Will it treat deep neck lines?

Neck Hydration improves skin quality. For deeper horizontal lines, we recommend Tech Neck, or a plan that combines both.

Can I do it with my face treatment?

Yes. Many clients treat face and neck in the same visit, so both look the same age.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable if you are allergic to fish or salmon, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have an active skin infection or cold sore in the area. Tell us about any autoimmune conditions or blood-thinning medications.

How should I prepare?

A skin test is required before your first treatment. Pause retinol and exfoliating acids on the neck for a few days. Wear a top that leaves your neck free. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

Small bumps settle within a day or two. For 24 hours, skip saunas, intense workouts and alcohol. Gentle skincare and daily sunscreen on the neck.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Skin looks more hydrated within days. Texture keeps improving with each session. Results vary by individual. Your provider will recommend how many sessions you need. Then maintenance as needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
