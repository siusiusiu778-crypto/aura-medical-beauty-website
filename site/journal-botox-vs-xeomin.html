Botox® vs Xeomin®: what’s the difference?

By [Author], AURA JournalMedically reviewed by [Provider name, NP]5 min readUpdated [date]
In this articleWhat’s the difference?What’s the sameHow we chooseWhat to expectThe bottom lineSee the treatment

Both are FDA-approved wrinkle relaxers that soften expression lines. So why do clinics offer two? Here is what our providers tell clients in consultation.

What’s the difference?

Botox® contains the active ingredient plus accessory proteins. Xeomin® is purified to the active ingredient alone, which is why it is sometimes called a “naked” formula.

Provider noteFor most people, results look the same. Placement and dose matter far more than the brand.

What’s the same

Both start working in about 3–5 days, reach full effect around day 14, and last about 3–4 months.

How we choose

We consider your history with each product, your goals and your budget. Your provider explains the options and gives you a clear quote at consultation.

What to expect

Treatment takes 10–20 minutes with no downtime. Stay upright for 4 hours and skip workouts for 24 hours.

The bottom line

Choose a provider, not a brand. A good plan uses the right product in the right places.
