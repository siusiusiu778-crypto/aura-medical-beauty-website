How it works

Know your skin first

Much of what shapes your skin sits below what the eye, or the mirror, can see. A medical-grade imaging system photographs your skin under different kinds of light to reveal hydration, pigment, redness and texture, layer by layer. A licensed provider then reads the results with you and recommends what to do first, and what can wait.

What we look at

Six things your skin tells us

  1. 01

    Hydration

    How well your skin holds moisture.

  2. 02

    Pigment and sun damage

    Visible spots and the hidden damage underneath.

  3. 03

    Redness and sensitivity

    Signs of a weakened barrier or irritation.

  4. 04

    Pores and texture

Your results guide everything from HydraFacial® to BBL™ Photofacial and Gold RF Microneedling.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    Your visit
    Tell us what you’d like to change and what you want to keep. We cleanse your skin and take a set of images under daylight, polarized and UV light.
  2. 02
    Your plan
    A licensed provider walks you through the results on screen, not just a score. You leave with a written plan: what to start with, timing and a clear quote. No obligation to book.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is it complimentary, and do I have to book?

Yes, for new clients.

No. You’ll leave with a written plan. Start the same day, or take it home and think it over.

Who is it for?

New clients, anyone unsure where to start, and returning clients who want to track their progress.

What happens to my photos?

They are stored securely in your private client record and used only to plan and track your treatments. We never share them without your consent.

How should I prepare for my visit?

Come with clean skin, or we’ll cleanse it for you. Skip self-tanner for a few days before. Avoid peels or lasers in the 7 days before, as they can change the reading.

How often should I come in?

Before your first treatment, then again over time to track your results.
