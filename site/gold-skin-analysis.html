FAQs

Good questions

Is it really complimentary?

Yes, for new clients. There is no obligation to book a treatment.

Who performs it?

A licensed provider reviews your results with you.

Do I need to remove makeup?

Bare skin gives the most accurate reading. We provide a gentle cleanser on arrival.

How often should I repeat it?

Every 3 months is ideal to track a treatment series or seasonal changes.

How much does it cost?

Every plan is different. The number of units, sessions and the right combination are decided with your provider at a complimentary consultation, and you’ll receive a clear quote before any treatment.
