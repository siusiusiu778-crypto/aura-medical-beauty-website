How it works

Energy, at the cellular level

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It helps turn food into energy and supports the cell’s own repair processes. NAD+ levels naturally decline with age. An IV drip delivers NAD+ directly into the bloodstream, as part of a wellness routine for energy, focus and recovery.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), a coenzyme your body already makes and uses in every cell, delivered by IV directly into the bloodstream. Available in 250 mg or 500 mg.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How does it feel?

You’ll feel a small pinch when the IV is placed. During the drip, some people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or tightness in the chest or stomach. These pass quickly when we slow the drip.

Why a health screening, and who should not have it?

IV therapy goes directly into your bloodstream, so we review your health history and medications before your first drip to make sure it’s right for you.

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for people with certain heart, kidney or liver conditions. Your health screening checks for this.

Is NAD+ part of AURA Prime?

Yes. AURA Prime members earn 50 Wellness Credits every month, and 500 credits can be redeemed for 1 NAD+ IV Drip.

What should I know before and after?

Before: Eat a light meal and drink plenty of water beforehand. Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily. Tell us about all medications and supplements you take.

After: There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. Keep drinking water for the rest of the day. Some people feel energized, others pleasantly tired.

When will I see results?

Many clients notice a difference in energy over the series. Results vary by individual. A series of 6 is a good place to start. Then maintenance as needed.
