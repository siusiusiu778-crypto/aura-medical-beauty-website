How we design it

Supervised, start to finish

  1. Screen A provider reviews your health history and medications.
  2. Dose 250 mg or 500 mg, chosen with you.
  3. Drip A slow infusion, adjusted for your comfort.
  4. Rest Water, snacks and a quiet lounge.

Aftercare

  • Drink water for the rest of the day.
  • Keep the bandage on for 1 hour.
  • Back to work or the gym right away.

Please wait if you…

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have kidney or heart conditions (we’ll check with your provider).
  • Are currently undergoing cancer treatment.

FAQs

Good questions

Who administers the drip?

A licensed nurse, under provider supervision.

Does it feel uncomfortable?

Some clients feel warmth or pressure. We slow the drip so you stay comfortable.

Is it covered by insurance?

No. NAD+ IV is a wellness service.

Can I use Wellness Credits?

Yes. 500 AURA Prime Wellness Credits = 1 NAD+ drip.

How much does it cost?

Every plan is different. The number of units, sessions and the right combination are decided with your provider at a complimentary consultation, and you’ll receive a clear quote before any treatment.
