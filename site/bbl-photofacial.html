How it works

How BBL™ works for your skin

BBL™ (BroadBand Light) is an advanced intense pulsed light treatment. Gentle pulses of light are absorbed by pigment and by tiny visible blood vessels. Over the following days, sun spots and freckles darken and flake away and redness fades, revealing a clearer, more even tone. With regular treatments, skin also looks smoother and healthier.

BBL™ is FDA-cleared. Settings are tailored to your skin tone and concerns at every session.

Is it right for you?

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

Most clients feel a quick, warm snap with each pulse, like a light rubber band. A cool gel keeps you comfortable.

Is it right for my skin tone, or for melasma?

BBL™ works best on fair to medium skin tones. For deeper skin tones, we’ll recommend a safer option, such as Gold RF Microneedling.

We don’t recommend it. Heat-based light can make melasma worse. We’ll suggest a gentler plan at your consultation.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy, with a recent tan or sunburn, or while taking medications that make skin sensitive to light. Please reschedule if you have an active skin infection or cold sore in the area.

What should I know before and after?

Before: Avoid sun, tanning and self-tanner for 2 weeks. Tell us about any medications. Some, like aspirin and certain antibiotics, make skin more sensitive to light. Pause retinol and exfoliating acids for a few days. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin.

After: Mild redness and warmth usually fade within a few hours to a day. Spots darken, then flake off over about 7–10 days. Let them shed on their own. Use a cool compress if needed, and gentle, hydrating skincare. Wear SPF 30+ every day and avoid direct sun.

When will I see results?

Skin looks brighter as spots flake away, within one to two weeks. Tone becomes more even with each session. Results vary by individual. Most clients do a series of 3 treatments, a few weeks apart. Then occasional maintenance to keep new sun damage in check.
