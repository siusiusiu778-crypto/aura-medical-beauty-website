How we design it

Planned like design

We look at proportion, not just inches.

  1. Measure Photos, measurements and a pinch test of each area.
  2. Plan Which areas, in what order, for a balanced shape.
  3. Treat Four sessions, spaced for your schedule.
  4. Track Progress photos at weeks 4, 8 and 12.

Recommended for

Is this right for you?

  • Close to your goal weight with one stubborn area
  • Not ready for surgery
  • Want no downtime
  • Postpartum body goals (after you finish breastfeeding)

What to expect

Your timeline

Session 1

30 minutes, lie back and relax. Mild warmth or tingling.

Weeks 2–4

Your body begins to clear treated fat cells.

Weeks 8–12

Visible change in shape and how clothes fit.

Maintain

A steady weight keeps results.

Aftercare

  • Return to normal activity right away.
  • Drink plenty of water for the next few days.
  • Keep a steady routine of movement and diet.
  • Progress photos at weeks 4, 8 and 12.

Please wait if you…

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have a pacemaker or metal implant in the area.
  • Have a hernia or recent surgery in the area.

FAQs

Good questions

Is it a weight-loss treatment?

No. It reduces fat in targeted areas. It works best close to your goal weight.

Does it hurt?

Most clients feel warmth or tingling. You can read or nap.

When will I see results?

Change builds over 8–12 weeks as your body clears treated fat.
