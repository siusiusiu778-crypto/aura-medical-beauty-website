How it works

How filler works for your nose

Also called a liquid rhinoplasty. A small amount of hyaluronic acid filler is placed precisely along the bridge or tip to smooth a bump, lift the tip or straighten the line. It can’t make a nose smaller, but it can make it look smoother, straighter and more balanced with your face.

Smooths bumps

A small amount of filler along the bridge smooths a bump and straightens the line.

Lifts the tip

Placed precisely at the tip, it gives a subtle lift to a drooping tip.

Raises a low bridge

More definition between the eyes, for a straighter, more balanced line.

Balances the face

The nose is the center of the face. We plan it with your lips and chin, as one profile.

We use only hyaluronic acid filler for the nose, so it can be dissolved if needed. Using filler in the nose is an off-label use. We’ll explain the benefits and risks at your consultation.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • A small bump on the bridge
  • A low or flat bridge
  • A slightly drooping tip
  • Trying a change before considering surgery

Does not treat

  • Making the nose smaller or narrower
  • Breathing problems
  • A large hump or major asymmetry
  • Recent nose surgery (please consult us first)

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    Cross-linked hyaluronic acid (HA), a water-holding sugar your body already makes. The nose needs only a small amount of firm support along the bridge or tip. We use only HA here, so it can always be dissolved.
  2. 02
    Our approach
    We plan your nose with your whole profile, using small amounts, a careful technique and a follow-up after every treatment.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt? Any side effects?

We apply a numbing cream first, and most fillers also contain a local anesthetic. Most clients feel only light pressure.

Swelling, bruising, redness or tenderness are common and usually settle within a few days to a week. Less often: small lumps or unevenness, which can usually be corrected. Rarely, filler can block a blood vessel. This needs immediate care, so call us right away if you notice unusual pain, skin color changes or any change in your vision.

Can it make my nose smaller?

No. Filler adds, it doesn’t remove. But by smoothing a bump or lifting the tip, the nose can look smaller and more balanced.

Is it safe, and who should not have it?

The nose has delicate blood supply, so technique matters. We use only dissolvable hyaluronic acid filler, small amounts and a careful technique, and we check in with you after treatment.

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, if you have an active skin infection or cold sore in the area, or if you have a history of severe allergies to filler ingredients. Talk to us first about bleeding disorders, autoimmune conditions or recent procedures in the area.

What should I know before and after?

Before: For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless they are prescribed for you. Plan at least 2 weeks before a big event. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin.

After: Mild swelling, tenderness or bruising is normal for a few days. Use a cold compress as needed. Skip intense workouts, saunas and alcohol for 24–48 hours. Don’t press or massage the area unless we ask you to. Avoid glasses resting on the bridge for 2 weeks.

When will I see results?

Visible right away. How long it lasts depends on the product and your body. Results vary by individual. We see you for a check-in at about 2 weeks, then maintenance as needed.
