FAQs

Membership questions

When does the quarterly IPL or Glow Booster unlock?

After 3 consecutive months of membership, and every 3 months after that.

What if I don’t use my facial this month?

Monthly facials must be used within that month and don’t carry over.

Do Wellness Credits expire?

Credits keep adding up while you are a member. 500 credits can be redeemed for 1 NAD+ Wellness Treatment.

Can I get cash or a refund for unused perks?

Perks that haven’t been unlocked cannot be exchanged for cash or refunded.

How do I cancel?

After the 3-month minimum, give us 30 days’ notice at the front desk or by email.

What’s the difference between Glow Girl and Aura VIP?

Glow Girl is a monthly routine with set perks. Aura VIP is prepaid credit with bonus value you can use on any treatment, plus year-round perks.
