How it works

Firm, smooth, hydrated

Developed by BTL, Exion™ combines monopolar radiofrequency with targeted ultrasound. Together they gently warm the skin to support collagen and help it make more of its own hyaluronic acid, the molecule that keeps skin plump and hydrated. Treatment is planned by area: cheeks, eyes, forehead or neck.

Firms skin

Supports collagen for firmer, tighter skin.

Boosts hydration

Helps skin make its own hyaluronic acid.

Smooths lines

Softer fine lines and wrinkles.

Refines texture

Smoother, more even skin.

Exion™ is FDA-cleared.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

Most clients feel only gentle warmth, like a warm stone massage. There are no needles.

What are the side effects?

Mild redness, warmth or swelling that usually fades within hours. Serious side effects are rare.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, metal implants in the treatment area, or are pregnant. Please wait if you have broken or inflamed skin in the area. Talk to us first about recent filler or Botox® in the area, chronic conditions or skin medications.

How is Exion™ different from Thermage® FLX?

Thermage® FLX is a single, deeper tightening treatment, maintained once a year. Exion™ is gentler, done as a series, and focuses on firmness and hydration, which makes it a good fit for the eyes and neck. Many clients use both in one plan.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. It pairs well with EMFACE® for lift, Dermal Fillers for volume and Rejuran® for skin quality. We’ll plan the timing for you.

How should I prepare?

Come in with clean, makeup-free skin. Pause retinol and exfoliating acids for a few days. Avoid sunburn in the area. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. Mild redness or warmth fades within a few hours. Use gentle, hydrating skincare and daily sunscreen.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Skin often looks more radiant right away. Firmness and smoothness build over the following weeks and months. Results vary by individual. A series of 4 sessions, spaced over a few weeks. Treat one area or combine up to three: cheeks, eyes, forehead or neck.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
