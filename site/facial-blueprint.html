FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What happens during a Facial Blueprint consultation?

We start with your goals and review your health history, medications and past treatments. Then we look at your face from the front and the side, at rest and when you smile, and walk you through your proportions before recommending anything.

Do I need to know what treatment I want?

No. Many clients come in with one concern, like their lips or under-eyes. We look at your whole face and explain what will make the biggest difference, and what can wait.

Is it complimentary, and do I have to book a treatment?

Yes, the consultation is complimentary, and there is no obligation. You leave with your plan, and you can start when you’re ready.

Will I look overdone?

No. We plan around your own features and build gradually, in small amounts, for results that look natural and refined. Many hyaluronic acid fillers can also be dissolved if needed.

How is it different from a Gold Skin Analysis?

A Gold Skin Analysis reads the skin itself: hydration, pigment, redness and texture. Your Facial Blueprint reads proportion and structure. Many clients do both in the same visit.
