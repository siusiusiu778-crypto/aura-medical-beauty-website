How it works

How hyaluronic acid filler works for your chin

Hyaluronic acid (HA) filler is a smooth, cross-linked gel. For the chin, we use a firm HA with strong lift, placed close to the chin bone, where it adds structure and shape right away. Most HA fillers contain lidocaine for comfort, and HA can be dissolved if needed.

Results right away. No surgery.

Adds projection

A firm gel placed over the chin bone brings a recessed chin forward, so the lower face looks stronger and better supported.

Balances the profile

From the side, the tip of the nose, the lips and the chin come into line, so the nose looks in proportion and the lips sit in balance.

Refines the shape

Small amounts shape the chin softer and rounder, or more defined, to suit your features and your style.

Supports the jawline

Where needed, we blend the chin into the jaw for one clean line from chin to ear, so it never looks added on.

Hyaluronic acid fillers are FDA-approved. We’ll tell you which product we recommend for you, and why.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • A chin that sits back in profile
  • A short or soft chin
  • Wanting balance between nose, lips and chin
  • A subtle change without surgery

Does not treat

  • Fullness under the chin
  • Major bite or jaw alignment issues
  • Very loose skin on the lower face

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    Cross-linked hyaluronic acid (HA), a water-holding sugar your body already makes. For the chin we use a firm HA (high G′) that holds its shape over the bone. It contains lidocaine and can be dissolved if needed.
  2. 02
    Our approach
    We read your whole profile first, then place filler precisely so the chin looks natural at rest and when you smile. Results soften gradually over time.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt? Any side effects?

We apply a numbing cream first, and most fillers also contain a local anesthetic. Most clients feel only light pressure.

Swelling, bruising, redness or tenderness are common and usually settle within a few days to a week. Less often: small lumps or unevenness, which can usually be corrected. Rarely, filler can block a blood vessel. This needs immediate care, so call us right away if you notice unusual pain, skin color changes or any change in your vision.

Will it look natural?

Yes. We plan your chin around your whole profile, not a template, and build gradually. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved if needed.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, if you have an active skin infection or cold sore in the area, or if you have a history of severe allergies to filler ingredients. Talk to us first about bleeding disorders, autoimmune conditions or recent procedures in the area.

What should I know before and after?

Before: For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless they are prescribed for you. Plan at least 2 weeks before a big event. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin.

After: Mild swelling, tenderness or bruising is normal for a few days. Use a cold compress as needed. Skip intense workouts, saunas and alcohol for 24–48 hours. Don’t press or massage the area unless we ask you to.

When will I see results?

You’ll see the change right away. The final result settles in about 2 weeks. How long it lasts depends on the product and your body. Results vary by individual. We see you for a check-in at about 2 weeks, then maintenance as the filler gradually softens.
