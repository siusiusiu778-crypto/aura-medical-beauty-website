How it works

Wellness, from the mind

About the treatment

Settle in
You sit back in a comfortable chair, fully clothed. No numbing needed.
Gentle pulses
The applicator rests on your head. You’ll feel a light tapping and slight tingling on the scalp.
Back to your day
Each session takes under 30 minutes. You can work, drive and exercise right after.

EXOMIND™ is not a substitute for medical or mental health care. If you’re being treated for depression or anxiety, please keep taking your medication and talk with your prescriber before making any changes.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

No. There’s no numbing and you stay fully clothed. You’ll feel a light tapping and slight tingling on the scalp. Many clients relax so much they fall asleep.

Are there side effects?

There’s no downtime. A few people feel briefly lightheaded, tired, slightly red on the scalp, or emotional after a session. This passes within hours.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or when trying to conceive, with metal implants or electronic devices in the head (such as a brain stimulator, skull plate or cochlear implant), with a history of epilepsy or seizures, serious head injury, brain tumor or recent brain surgery, or during an acute psychiatric episode or substance use crisis.

Can I do it alongside therapy or medication?

Yes. EXOMIND™ is drug-free and can be part of your routine alongside therapy or medication. Please don’t stop or change any medication without talking to your prescriber.

How should I prepare?

Tell us about any medications and your health history, including seizures or implants. Remove metal hair accessories and earrings. Braces and necklaces are fine. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. A few people feel briefly lightheaded, tired or emotional, which passes within hours.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Many clients notice better sleep and less stress within the first few sessions. Mood and focus keep improving over the series. Results vary by individual. A series of 6 is a good place to start. Then maintenance sessions as needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
