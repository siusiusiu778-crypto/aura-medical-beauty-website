Injectables · Wrinkle Relaxers

Smooth, not frozen

Wrinkle relaxers soften the muscles that crease your skin when you frown, squint or raise your brows. Placed well, you still look like you, just more rested.

Forehead lines

Horizontal lines when you raise your brows.

Typically 10–20 units

Frown lines (11s)

Vertical lines between the brows.

Typically 20 units

Crow’s feet

Fan lines at the outer eyes when you smile.

Typically 12 units per side

Jaw slimming

Softens a square jaw from a strong masseter.

Planned at consultation

How we design it

Mapped to your face

The product matters less than where and how much. This is where design comes in.

  1. Assess We watch your face at rest and in motion to see which muscles create each line.
  2. Map Injection points are marked to your anatomy, balancing brows and eyes.
  3. Dose Your provider chooses Botox® or Xeomin® and the units for each point.
  4. Refine A 2-week check to fine-tune, included with your first treatment.

Recommended for

Is this right for you?

  • First lines that stay after your face relaxes
  • Looking tired or stern when you’re not
  • Preventing deeper lines from setting in
  • A square jaw from clenching
  • Anyone who wants subtle, natural movement

What to expect

Aftercare

  • Stay upright for 4 hours.
  • Skip workouts, saunas and facial massage for 24 hours.
  • Don’t rub or press the treated areas today.
  • Come back at day 14 for your check.

Please wait if you…

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Have a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis.
  • Have an infection or inflammation at the treatment site.
  • Have had a reaction to a botulinum toxin before.

FAQs

Good questions

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe a quick pinch. We use a very fine needle and can apply ice first.

Will I look frozen?

Not when it is dosed well. We aim for softer lines with natural movement.

Should I choose Botox® or Xeomin®?

Both work very well. Your provider recommends one based on your history and goals.

What should I avoid afterwards?

Stay upright for 4 hours and skip workouts, saunas and facial massage for 24 hours.

Who should not have it?

Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, or with certain neuromuscular conditions. We review your health history first.

How much does it cost?

Every plan is different. The number of units, sessions and the right combination are decided with your provider at a complimentary consultation, and you’ll receive a clear quote before any treatment.
