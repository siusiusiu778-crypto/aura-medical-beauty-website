How the Signature Facial works for your skin

Every Signature Facial begins with a look at your skin that day. Your esthetician tailors each step, with professional-grade skincare, gentle extractions where needed and a slow massage of the face, neck and shoulders. It is real care for your skin, and an hour to rest.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How is it different from HydraFacial®?

The Signature Facial is a hands-on, relaxing facial with massage, tailored on the day. HydraFacial® uses a device for a deeper cleanse and extraction. Many clients alternate between the two.

Is it okay for sensitive skin?

Yes. Mild to moderately sensitive skin does well, and the facial can help calm redness and support the skin barrier.

Will my skin peel?

Usually not. Mild pinkness for a few hours is normal, and light flaking within a week is just natural renewal.

What should I know before and after?

Before: Pause acids, retinol, high-strength vitamin C and scrubs for 3–5 days. Skip waxing, lasers and radiofrequency treatments for 1 week. If you’ve taken isotretinoin, please wait 6 months after your last dose. Come makeup-free if you can.

After: No downtime. Mild pinkness fades within a few hours. For 24 hours, skip makeup, saunas and intense workouts. For 3–7 days, keep your routine gentle and wear sunscreen.

When will I see results?

Skin looks brighter, softer and clearer right away. Tone and texture look more even over the next 3–7 days. Results vary by individual. About once a month is ideal. Every 6 weeks for sensitive skin, every 2–3 weeks for oily skin.
