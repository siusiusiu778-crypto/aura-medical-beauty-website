How it works

How PRP & PRF work for your under-eyes

The skin under the eyes is thin, so blood vessels and shadows show through easily. PRP (platelet-rich plasma) and PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) are prepared from a small sample of your own blood and concentrate the growth factors your body uses to repair tissue. Placed under the eyes, they support collagen and thicker, healthier skin, so dark circles look softer and the area looks less tired.

Nothing added but you.

Softens dark circles

Your own growth factors support thin under-eye skin, so less of the shadow shows through.

Thicker skin

PRP and PRF support collagen in the thin skin under the eyes, without adding volume.

Smoother texture

Softer fine lines and crepey skin under the eyes.

Natural results

Made from a small sample of your own blood, for a fresher, more rested look.

Is it right for you?

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    Your own platelet-rich plasma (PRP) or platelet-rich fibrin (PRF), from a small blood draw. It improves thin under-eye skin without adding volume, and since it is your own, there is no allergy risk to the plasma itself.
  2. 02
    The treatment
    A small blood sample is taken from your arm and spun in a centrifuge. After numbing, it is placed carefully under the eyes.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt? Any side effects?

We apply numbing cream first. Most clients feel light pressure and a few quick pricks.

Swelling, redness and bruising under the eyes are common for a few days. You may also have a small bruise where the blood was drawn.

Is it the same as under-eye filler?

No. Filler adds volume to hollows. PRP and PRF don’t add volume. They improve the quality of the skin itself. Some clients benefit from both.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable if you have a blood or platelet disorder, are on blood-thinning medication, have an active infection, or are pregnant or breastfeeding.

What should I know before and after?

Before: Drink plenty of water and eat a meal beforehand. For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless prescribed. Tell us about any blood-thinning medications.

After: Mild swelling or bruising under the eyes is normal for a few days. Use a cold compress if needed. For 24 hours, skip makeup on the area, saunas and intense workouts.

When will I see results?

Results build gradually as collagen improves, over weeks to months. The under-eye area looks brighter and less tired. Results vary by individual. Your provider will recommend how many sessions you need. Then maintenance as needed.
