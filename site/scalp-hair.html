How it works

A healthier scalp, fuller-looking hair

Healthy hair starts with a healthy scalp. We offer two treatments, chosen for your scalp and goals. PRP (platelet-rich plasma) is prepared from a small sample of your own blood and concentrates the growth factors your body uses to repair tissue. Rejuran® hydrates and supports a stressed, dry scalp.

Your scalp is skin, too.

01

PRP supports weak, thinning hair from the root.

Your own biology

Using PRP for the scalp is an off-label use of an FDA-cleared preparation system. We’ll explain the benefits and limits at your consultation.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • Early or gradual hair thinning
  • Hair shedding from stress or after pregnancy
  • A dry, flaky or sensitive scalp
  • Wanting a non-surgical option

Does not treat

  • Advanced baldness with no active follicles
  • Hair loss from an untreated medical condition
  • Active scalp infection
  • Blood or platelet disorders (PRP)

Pairs wellMany clients combine PRP and Rejuran® in one plan, for both fuller-looking hair and a healthier scalp.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    PRP
    Platelet-rich plasma from a small sample of your own blood, spun in a centrifuge to concentrate platelets and their growth factors.
  2. 02
    Rejuran®
    Polynucleotides (PN) derived from salmon that support the skin’s own repair and hydration. A skin test is done first.
  3. 03
    Which one?
    PRP is usually recommended for thinning hair. Rejuran® suits a dry, stressed or sensitive scalp. We’ll recommend one, or both, at your consultation.
  4. 04
    The treatment
    1. Consultation. We look at your scalp and hair pattern and choose PRP, Rejuran® or both.
    2. Prepare. For PRP, a small blood draw is spun to separate the platelet-rich plasma. For Rejuran®, a skin test comes first.
    3. Treat the scalp. Fine, even placement across the thinning areas, with numbing for comfort.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

We use numbing to keep you comfortable. Most clients feel light pressure and quick pricks.

PRP or Rejuran®: which is right for me?

PRP is usually recommended for thinning hair. Rejuran® suits a dry, stressed or sensitive scalp. Some clients combine both.

How long until I see results?

Hair grows slowly, so expect to see changes over a few months, not weeks.

Is PRP safe?

PRP is made from your own blood, so there is no risk of an allergic reaction to the plasma itself. Mild tenderness, swelling or a small bruise from the blood draw can occur.

Who should not have it?

PRP is not suitable if you have a blood or platelet disorder, are on blood-thinning medication, or have an active infection. Rejuran® is not suitable if you are allergic to fish or salmon. Please tell us if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

How should I prepare?

Wash your hair the day before, and skip styling products. For PRP, eat well and drink plenty of water beforehand. Tell us about blood thinners, supplements or recent hair treatments. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

Mild tenderness on the scalp is normal for a day or two. Wait until the next day to wash your hair. Skip hair coloring, saunas and intense workouts for a few days.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Hair changes slowly, so results take a few months to show. Most clients notice less shedding and fuller-looking hair over a series. Results vary by individual. Your provider will recommend how many sessions you need. Then maintenance as needed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
