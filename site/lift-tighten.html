Why skin loosens

Aging starts below the surface

Skin is the last layer to show it. That’s why we treat from the deepest layer up, not just the line you see.

  1. Bone and ligament support thins first.
  2. Fat pads and muscles begin to settle.
  3. Skin loses collagen and elasticity.

FAQ

Good to know

Still have questions? Ask us at your free consultation.

Does it hurt?

EMFACE feels like warmth with a light tapping. Thermage feels like short bursts of heat, and we adjust the level for your comfort. Most clients don’t need numbing.

When will I see results?

Many clients notice a firmer look within a few weeks. Collagen keeps building for about 3 months, so results improve over time.

How long do results last?

It depends on your skin and the treatment. Thermage is usually repeated once a year; EMFACE is done as a series of 4, then maintained.

Can I go back to work right after?

Yes. You may look slightly flushed for a few hours. You can wear makeup the same day.

Can I combine it with Botox or fillers?

Yes, and many clients do. Your provider will plan the order and timing so each treatment works at its best.
