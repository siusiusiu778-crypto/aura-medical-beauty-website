How it works

How Facial Harmonization works for your face

No face is perfectly symmetrical, and that’s part of what makes it yours. But when one cheek or jowl holds more fullness than the other, the face can look uneven. Facial Harmonization uses small, precise fat-dissolving injections on the fuller side only, so both sides look more balanced.

Balanced, not identical.

Balances both sides

We compare both sides at rest and when you smile, with photos, then treat only the fuller side.

Targets one area

Small, precise fat-dissolving injections are placed in the fuller cheek or jowl on one side only.

Refines the lower face

Over the following weeks the treated fat cells are cleared, and that side looks less full.

Natural-looking

Balanced, not identical: small, careful changes that keep your face your own.

KYBELLA® is FDA-approved for fat under the chin. Using deoxycholic acid elsewhere on the face is off-label. We’ll explain the benefits and risks at your consultation.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • One side of the face fuller than the other
  • Uneven fullness at the cheek or jowl
  • Small, defined areas of fat
  • A non-surgical option

Does not treat

  • Asymmetry from bone structure
  • Asymmetry from a stronger jaw muscle on one side
  • Loose or sagging skin
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding

Pairs wellTo treat both sides, see Lower Face Tightening + Lift. For a stronger jaw muscle on one side, see Jaw Slimming. For structure, see Dermal Fillers.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    Deoxycholic acid, the active ingredient in KYBELLA®, a molecule your body already makes to break down fat. It destroys fat cells in the treated area, and the body clears them over the following weeks.
  2. 02
    Why one side
    Treating only the fuller side brings the two sides closer together, without changing the side that is already in balance.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Will my face be perfectly symmetrical?

No face is, and we don’t aim for that. The goal is balance, so the two sides look more even and natural.

What are the side effects and swelling like?

Swelling, bruising, pain, numbness, redness and firmness in the area are common and temporary. Rarely, a nerve near the jaw can be irritated and cause an uneven smile, or there can be trouble swallowing. These usually resolve, but call us right away if you notice them.

Noticeable swelling is normal and is part of how the treatment works. It usually settles over several days to a couple of weeks, so plan around important events.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, with an infection in the area, or where fullness comes from loose skin or another cause. Tell us about any past facial surgery or treatments in the area.

What should I know before and after?

Before: For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless prescribed. Plan at least 2 weeks before a big event, to allow for swelling. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin.

After: Swelling, tenderness, bruising and numbness are normal and can last from several days to a couple of weeks. Use cold compresses as needed, and sleep with your head slightly raised the first nights. Skip intense workouts for a few days.

When will I see results?

Results appear once swelling settles, and keep improving over the following weeks. Results vary by individual. Some areas need more than one session, spaced out so swelling can fully settle. Your provider will recommend how many sessions you need.
