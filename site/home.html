The AURA Experience

From your first conversation to your follow-up, every step is about you.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Tell us what you’d like to change, and what you want to keep. Complimentary, with no pressure to book.

  2. 02

    Face Mapping

    A full-face assessment and Gold Skin Analysis, looking at proportion, skin and how your face moves.

  3. 03

    Your Plan

    A written plan with options and timing, designed around your features and your schedule.

  4. 04

    Treatment

    Performed by a licensed professional in a private suite, with care to keep your results natural.

  5. 05

    Follow-Up

    A check-in after your treatment to make sure you love your results, and a plan for what comes next.
