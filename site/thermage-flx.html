How it works

How Thermage® FLX works for your skin

Developed by Solta Medical, Thermage® FLX uses monopolar radiofrequency to gently heat the deeper layers of the skin. Existing collagen contracts right away for an instant firming effect, and new collagen keeps building for months after. No needles, no incisions, no downtime.

Smooths lines

Softens forehead lines, crow’s feet, smile lines and neck lines.

Lifts and firms

Tightens loose skin for a firmer, more lifted look.

Refines contours

A cleaner, more defined lower face.

Renews skin quality

Smoother texture, refined pores and a healthy glow as collagen renews.

FDA-cleared for the non-invasive treatment of wrinkles, including around the eyes and on the upper and lower eyelids.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • All skin tones and types
  • Early signs of loosening
  • Fine lines and crepey skin
  • A gradual, natural change without surgery
  • Sensitive skin in a stable phase

Does not treat

  • Volume loss
  • Sun spots and pigmentation
  • Active acne or breakouts
  • Advanced sagging that needs surgery

For volume, pair it with RADIESSE®. For tone, see BBL™ Photofacial.

The technology

Precise, even, comfortable

  1. Even energy deliveryA patented treatment tip spreads radiofrequency evenly into the skin, reducing hot spots.
  2. AccuREP™ technologyReads your skin with every pulse and adjusts the energy, so each pulse is precise.
  3. Built-in coolingCools the skin surface while the deeper layers warm, for a more comfortable treatment.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

Most clients feel warmth and a deep, brief pressure with each pulse. The tip cools your skin, and your provider can adjust the energy to your comfort.

What are the side effects?

Mild redness, warmth or swelling that usually settles within hours to a few days. Less often: tingling, small bumps or temporary darkening of the skin. Blisters or burns are rare.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device. Please wait if you are pregnant, or have broken or inflamed skin in the area. Talk to us first about metal implants, chronic conditions, filler or tattoos in the area.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes, as part of one plan. It is often paired with EMFACE® for added lift and Dermal Fillers to restore softness. We leave at least 2 weeks between Thermage® FLX and peels, lasers, microneedling or injectables.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
