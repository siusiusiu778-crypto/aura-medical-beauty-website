How it works

Glow that lasts

SKINVIVE™ by JUVÉDERM® is a hyaluronic acid microdroplet treatment. Tiny droplets of a smooth HA gel are placed just beneath the surface of the cheeks, where they improve skin smoothness and hydration from within. It doesn’t change the shape of your face. It improves the quality of the skin itself.

Skin quality, not volume.

Smoother cheeks

A finer, smoother skin surface.

Lasting hydration

Hydration that holds, from within.

A natural glow

Radiant skin without a filled look.

One treatment

Results from a single visit, with optional touch-ups.

SKINVIVE™ is FDA-approved to improve skin smoothness of the cheeks in adults over 21.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    Material
    Hyaluronic acid (HA), a sugar your skin already makes that holds water, in a smooth, light gel made for the skin’s surface layers.
  2. 02
    Why it suits the cheeks
    The gel is placed as tiny droplets just beneath the surface, so it improves smoothness and hydration without adding volume or changing your features.
  3. 03
    Comfort
    SKINVIVE™ contains lidocaine, a local anesthetic, mixed into the gel.
  4. 04
    Reversible
    As an HA product, it can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase if ever needed.
  5. 05
    The treatment
    1. Plan. We look at your skin and mark the areas of the cheeks to treat.
    2. Cleanse and numb. Numbing cream and the lidocaine in the gel keep you comfortable.
    3. Microdroplets. Tiny droplets of HA gel are placed evenly across the cheeks.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

We apply numbing cream, and the gel contains lidocaine. Most clients feel quick, light pricks.

Will my face look fuller?

No. SKINVIVE™ is placed as tiny droplets close to the surface to improve skin quality, not to add volume.

What are the side effects?

Redness, bumps, swelling, bruising, tenderness, firmness, itching or discoloration at the treatment sites are common and usually resolve within a week. Rarely, an HA product can block a blood vessel. Call us right away if you notice unusual pain or skin color changes.

How is it different from Rejuran®?

SKINVIVE™ is a single hyaluronic acid treatment for cheek smoothness that lasts for months. Rejuran® is a series that supports the skin’s own repair. We’ll recommend the right one for your skin.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable if you are under 21, pregnant or breastfeeding, have a history of severe allergies, or have an active skin infection in the area.

How should I prepare?

For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless prescribed. Plan at least 2 weeks before a big event. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

Redness, small bumps, swelling or bruising are common and usually settle within a week. Skip makeup for the rest of the day. For 24 hours, skip intense workouts, saunas and alcohol.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Skin looks smoother and more radiant within weeks. Results can last up to 6 months with optimal treatment. Results vary by individual. It’s one treatment, with an optional touch-up. Then maintenance as the results gradually soften.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
