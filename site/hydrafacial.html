How it works

Clean, clear, hydrated

HydraFacial® uses a patented vortex technology to deep-clean and exfoliate, gently lift impurities out of your pores, and infuse your skin with serums rich in antioxidants, peptides and hyaluronic acid. FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

No. Most clients find it relaxing. You’ll feel a cool, gentle suction, a little like a light massage.

Deluxe or Platinum: what’s the difference?

Deluxe is the full HydraFacial® with a targeted serum chosen for your skin, hand-finished extractions and LED light therapy. Platinum adds lymphatic drainage first, to reduce puffiness and help skin detox, for a brighter, more sculpted finish.

Is it okay for sensitive skin?

Most sensitive skin does well. We adjust the suction and serums to your skin. If your skin is red, stinging or recently treated, we may start with a Barrier Recovery Facial instead.

Will my skin peel?

Almost never. You may see a little redness that fades quickly.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. It pairs well with many skin and laser treatments, and we’ll plan the timing for you. If you’re having filler or Botox®, we’ll schedule your facial a few days before, or at least 2 weeks after.

How should I prepare?

Pause retinol and strong acid products for 3–5 days. Reschedule if you have a sunburn, broken skin or an active breakout flare. Tell us if you’re pregnant, on prescription skincare, or had recent treatments. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. For 24 hours, skip saunas, steam rooms and intense workouts. For 48–72 hours, pause acids, retinol and exfoliating treatments. Gentle cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen every day.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Hydration and glow are visible right away. Pores, fine lines and breakouts improve with regular treatments. Results vary by individual. Every 4–6 weeks works well as ongoing skin care. A series of 4 is a good place to start.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
