How it works

How HydraFacial® works for your skin

HydraFacial® uses a patented vortex technology to deep-clean and exfoliate, gently lift impurities out of your pores, and infuse your skin with serums rich in antioxidants, peptides and hyaluronic acid. Five steps in one visit, gentle enough for most skin types, and you can go straight back to your day.

The monthly reset for your skin.

Cleanses and exfoliates

A swirling vortex cleanse and a gentle acid peel loosen dead skin and buildup, with no scrubbing.

Lifts out congestion

Gentle vacuum extraction draws blackheads, oil and debris out of the pores around the nose.

Floods skin with hydration

Serums with hyaluronic acid, antioxidants and peptides are infused into freshly cleared skin, for a plump, dewy feel.

Glows right away

Skin looks brighter and smoother as you leave, finished with LED light and no downtime.

Performed with genuine HydraFacial® serums and single-use tips.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

No. Most clients find it relaxing. You’ll feel a cool, gentle suction, a little like a light massage.

Deluxe or Platinum: what’s the difference?

Deluxe is the full HydraFacial® with a targeted serum chosen for your skin, hand-finished extractions and LED light therapy. Platinum adds lymphatic drainage first, to reduce puffiness and help skin detox, for a brighter, more sculpted finish.

Is it okay for sensitive skin?

Most sensitive skin does well. We adjust the suction and serums to your skin. If your skin is red, stinging or recently treated, we may start with a Barrier Recovery Facial instead.

What should I know before and after?

Before: Pause retinol and strong acid products for 3–5 days. Reschedule if you have a sunburn, broken skin or an active breakout flare. Tell us if you’re pregnant, on prescription skincare, or had recent treatments.

After: There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. For 24 hours, skip saunas, steam rooms and intense workouts. For 48–72 hours, pause acids, retinol and exfoliating treatments. Gentle cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen every day.

When will I see results?

Hydration and glow are visible right away. Pores, fine lines and breakouts improve with regular treatments. Results vary by individual. Every 4–6 weeks works well as ongoing skin care. A series of 4 is a good place to start.
