How it works

A longer neck, softer shoulders

The trapezius is the muscle that runs from the base of the skull across the top of the shoulders. When it is bulky or constantly tense, the shoulders look high and broad and the neck looks short. A small amount of Botox® or Xeomin® relaxes the muscle so it slims over time, for a softer, more graceful line from the neck to the shoulders.

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    Botulinum toxin type A (Botox® Cosmetic or Xeomin®), which temporarily relaxes a targeted muscle. As the trapezius is used less, it gradually slims, softening the slope from the neck to the shoulders.
  2. 02
    The treatment
    A skin test comes first. We map points on each side of the neck and shoulder line, then place a few quick injections.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt? Any side effects?

Most clients feel only quick pinches. No numbing is usually needed.

Mild soreness, tenderness or a small bruise at the injection points are most common. Rarely, the effect can spread and cause temporary weakness or discomfort in the neck or shoulders.

Will it make my shoulders weak?

You may notice slight fatigue when lifting heavy things over your head at first. Everyday movement isn’t affected. Tell us if you lift heavy weights or do overhead sports, so we can plan around it.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, if you have a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis, or if you’ve had an allergic reaction to botulinum toxin.

What should I know before and after?

Before: A skin test is done before your first treatment. For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, unless prescribed. Wear a top that leaves your shoulders easy to reach.

After: There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. Skip massage on the area and intense workouts for 24 hours. Mild soreness or a small bruise can appear and fades within days.

When will I see results?

The muscle relaxes over the first weeks, and the shoulder line slims gradually. Results last several months. Results vary by individual. Repeat as the effect wears off, usually a few times a year.
