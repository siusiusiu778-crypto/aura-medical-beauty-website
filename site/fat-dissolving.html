How it works

Small areas, precise results

Fat Dissolving uses deoxycholic acid, the active ingredient in KYBELLA®. Deoxycholic acid is a molecule your body already makes to break down dietary fat. Placed into a small pocket of fat, it breaks down the membranes of fat cells, and the body clears them naturally over the following weeks. Once destroyed, those fat cells can no longer store fat.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt? Any side effects?

We use numbing first. You may feel warmth, stinging or tingling during treatment, which settles quickly.

Swelling, bruising, tenderness, numbness, redness and firmness in the area are common and temporary. Rarely, there can be skin irritation, ulceration or nerve irritation. Call us if anything worries you.

Is the fat gone for good?

Treated fat cells are destroyed and can no longer store fat. Keeping a stable weight helps your result last.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, with an infection in the area, or for larger areas of fat or loose skin.

What should I know before and after?

Before: For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless prescribed. Plan around swelling: avoid treating right before an event. Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

After: Swelling, tenderness, bruising and numbness are normal and can last from several days to a couple of weeks. Use cold compresses as needed and wear loose clothing. Skip intense workouts for a few days.

When will I see results?

Swelling settles first, then results appear gradually over the following weeks. Results vary by individual. Most areas need more than one session, spaced out so swelling can fully settle. Your provider will recommend how many sessions you need.
