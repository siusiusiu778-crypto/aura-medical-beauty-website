01 · The AURA Aesthetic

Trends fade. Your style stays.

There is no single standard of beauty. Before we plan any treatment, we read your facial style across three dimensions, then design to bring it forward, never to replace it.

Scale

DelicateBold

The size and fullness of your features and contours.

Line

SoftStructured

Whether your contours read curved and soft, or angular and defined.

Contrast

SereneStriking

The presence of your features, from calm and harmonious to vivid and striking.

How we read your face

  1. 01
    Thirds and fifths.How the upper, middle and lower face, and the width of your features, relate.
  2. 02
    Profile and alignment.Brow, nose, lips and chin, and how they line up from the side.
  3. 03
    Light and shadow.The highlight points that catch light, and the soft hollows between them.
  4. 04
    Movement.How your face moves when you talk, smile and frown.

Look like yourself on your best day.

Precision over perfection. Small changes, placed where they matter. Your style is mapped together with you at your consultation.

04 · Journal & FAQ

Good questions.

Answers to what we're asked most. Can't find yours? Text us anytime.

What happens at my first visit?

We start with a consultation. We review your health history, look at your skin and features, and talk about what you'd like to change. Then we recommend a plan, and you decide what to do and when.

Is the consultation complimentary?

Yes. Your consultation, including a Gold Skin Analysis, is complimentary.

I don't know which treatment I need. Where do I start?

That's what the consultation is for. You can also browse by goal on our homepage, from skin tightening to glow and hydration.

Will I still look like myself?

Yes. Our approach is refined, never overdone. We make small, precise changes that bring your own features forward.

Is there downtime?

Most of our treatments have little or no downtime, and many fit into a lunch break. Your provider will tell you what to expect before every treatment.

Who should not have treatment?

Some treatments aren't suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or with certain health conditions or medications. We review all of this at your consultation.

How much do treatments cost?

It depends on your plan. You'll get a clear quote at your consultation, before any treatment.

Do you offer a membership?

Yes. AURA Prime is our membership for clients who want ongoing care. Ask us at your visit.

Do you offer consultations in Mandarin?

Yes. All consultations are available in English and Mandarin.
