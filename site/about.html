About AURA Institute

The light wasalways yours.

A medical aesthetics and wellness clinic at Rockefeller Center, New York.

Book a Consultation →

AURA Institute offers face design, skin treatments, body contouring and wellness therapies in a private suite on Fifth Avenue. Every plan is built around your own features, for results that look natural and feel like you.

Who we are

Look like yourself on your best day.

An aura is the light a person carries: the glow others see, and the confidence you feel. AURA Institute was founded to bring out both. We combine proven medical aesthetic treatments with wellness care, and plan every visit around your whole face and how you want to feel.

Our standards

Safety, in every detail

  1. 01

    A full consultation first

    We review your health history, medications and past treatments before recommending anything.

  2. 02

    Skin testing when needed

    For treatments that call for it, we test your skin before your first session.

  3. 03

    Sterile, single-use supplies

    Needles and tips are single-use and opened in front of you.

  4. 04

    Every product recorded

    We record the product and lot number of every injectable, and you are welcome to see the box before treatment.

  5. 05

    A follow-up after every treatment

    We check in on your results and recovery, and plan what comes next.

  6. 06

    Prepared for the unexpected

    Our team follows set safety protocols and keeps emergency supplies on site.
