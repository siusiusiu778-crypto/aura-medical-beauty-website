AURA Institute

AURA Institute is a medical aesthetics and wellness clinic in a private suite at Rockefeller Center. We offer injectables, skin tightening, skin treatments, body contouring and wellness care, with every plan built around your own features for results that look natural and feel like you.

Who we are

Look like yourself on your best day.

An aura is the light a person carries: the glow others see, and the confidence you feel. AURA Institute was created to bring out both. We bring advanced technology, precise injectables and wellness care together under one roof, and plan every visit around your whole face, not a single area. Consultations are available in English and Mandarin.
