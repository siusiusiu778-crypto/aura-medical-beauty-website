How it works

How jaw slimming works for your lower face

The masseter is the chewing muscle at the angle of the jaw. When it is strong, from genetics, clenching or grinding, the lower face looks wide and square. Relaxing the masseter with Botox® or Xeomin® lets the muscle slim over the following weeks, for a softer, more tapered jawline. Many clients also notice the jaw feels less tense.

Slimmer, not changed.

Relaxes the masseter

Botox® or Xeomin® is placed at a few points in the masseter, the chewing muscle at the angle of the jaw.

Slims the lower face

As the relaxed muscle is used less, it slims over the following weeks, so the lower face looks narrower.

Softens a square angle

Less bulk at the back of the jaw, for a smoother, more tapered line from ear to chin.

A softer, tapered shape

The lower face comes into proportion with your cheekbones, and many clients notice the jaw feels less tense.

Botox® Cosmetic and Xeomin® are FDA-approved for certain facial lines. Using them in the masseter is off-label. We’ll explain the benefits and risks at your consultation.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • A square or wide lower face from a strong jaw muscle
  • Jaw clenching or tension
  • Wanting a softer, slimmer face shape
  • A non-surgical option

Does not treat

  • Fullness from fat along the jaw
  • Loose skin at the jawline
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Neuromuscular conditions

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    The material
    Botulinum toxin type A (Botox® Cosmetic or Xeomin®), a purified protein that temporarily relaxes a muscle. The relaxed masseter is used less and gradually slims, so nothing is added.
  2. 02
    The treatment
    A skin test comes first. We feel the muscle while you clench, mark points on each side for balance, then place a few quick injections.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Will it affect chewing?

You can eat normally. Hard or chewy foods may feel slightly more tiring at first.

What are the side effects?

Mild redness, small bumps, tenderness or a small bruise at the injection points are common and fade quickly. Some people get a mild headache. Rarely, the effect can spread and cause a drooping brow or eyelid, which is temporary. Call us if anything concerns you.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding, if you have a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis, if you’ve had an allergic reaction to botulinum toxin, or if there is an infection in the area.

What should I know before and after?

Before: A skin test is done before your first treatment. For 7 days, avoid alcohol and supplements that can increase bruising, such as fish oil, vitamin E and ibuprofen, unless prescribed. Come in with clean, makeup-free skin.

After: There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. Don’t massage the jaw for the rest of the day. Skip intense workouts until the next day.

When will I see results?

The muscle slims gradually over several weeks. Results last a few months, and often longer with repeat treatments. Results vary by individual. Repeat as the effect wears off.
