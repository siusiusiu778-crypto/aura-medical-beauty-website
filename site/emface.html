How it works

Two layers, one treatment

Most devices treat skin or muscle. EMFACE® treats both at the same time: radiofrequency warms the skin to boost collagen and elastin, while muscle stimulation tones the small muscles that hold your face up. Together they lift and firm, with nothing that breaks the skin.

Forehead lines

Softer lines without freezing your expression.

Brows & cheeks

A gentle lift where volume has dropped.

Jawline

A more defined lower face and profile.

Needle-shy clients

Results with no injections at all.

FDA-cleared radiofrequency and electrical muscle stimulation for facial toning and lifting.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • Early forehead and brow drop
  • Soft volume loss in the cheeks
  • A less defined jawline
  • Needle-shy clients
  • Maintaining results between other treatments

Does not treat

  • Deep, static wrinkles
  • Significant volume loss
  • Loose, excess skin that needs a surgical lift
  • Active skin infection in the treatment area

For deeper lines, pair it with Botox® & Xeomin®. For looser skin, see Thermage® FLX.

The technology

Two signals, one session

  1. RadiofrequencyWarms the deeper layers of the skin to boost collagen and elastin.
  2. HIFES muscle stimulationTones the small muscles that hold your face up, the same muscles a facial exercise would reach.
  3. Continuous temperature controlMonitors the skin surface throughout, so each pulse stays safe and comfortable.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe warmth and a tapping sensation. No numbing is needed, and your provider adjusts the level to your comfort.

When will I see results?

Some clients notice a lifted look after the first sessions. Results build over the series and continue for weeks afterward.

What are the side effects?

Mild warmth or light redness for a few hours. Serious side effects are rare.

Who should not have it?

It is not recommended during pregnancy or with metal or electronic implants in the face, including a pacemaker. Your provider will review your health history at consultation.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. EMFACE® pairs well with Wrinkle Relaxers and Thermage® FLX. Your provider will plan the timing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
