How it works

Lift the face, without needles

Developed by BTL, EMFACE® is the first treatment to work on skin and the facial muscles together. Applicators rest on the forehead and cheeks. Radiofrequency warms the skin to support collagen, while gentle muscle stimulation tones the muscles that hold your face up. No needles, no downtime, and you can return to your day right away.

Lifts the brows

A subtle lift for a more open, rested look.

Lifts the cheeks

More support in the mid-face.

Defines the jawline

A firmer, more toned lower face.

Smooths lines

Softer lines as skin and muscle improve together.

EMFACE® is FDA-cleared.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does it feel like?

You’ll feel warmth and a light tapping or tightening as the muscles contract. Most clients find it relaxing, and the energy can be adjusted for comfort.

What are the side effects?

Mild redness, warmth or tingling that fades quickly. Some clients feel mild muscle soreness for a day. Serious side effects are rare.

Who should not have it?

It is not suitable if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, metal implants in the treatment area, or are pregnant. Please wait if you have broken or inflamed skin in the area. Talk to us first about recent filler or Botox® in the area, chronic conditions or skin medications.

Can I combine it with Botox® or filler?

Yes, as part of one plan. EMFACE® tones the muscles while Botox® relaxes others, so we plan the order and timing for you. It pairs well with Dermal Fillers for volume and Thermage® FLX for skin tightening.

How should I prepare?

Come in with clean, makeup-free skin. Remove jewelry from the face and neck. Tell us about any recent filler or Botox® so we can plan the timing. Your provider will review your health history and these instructions with you before treatment.

What is aftercare like?

There’s no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. Mild redness or warmth fades within an hour or so. You may feel a little muscle tenderness, like after a workout.

When will I see results, and how often should I come in?

Many clients notice a change after a few sessions. Results continue to improve for up to about 3 months after the last session. Results vary by individual. A series of 4 sessions, each about 20 minutes, then occasional maintenance.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your plan. You’ll get a clear quote at your complimentary consultation.
