How it works

Two layers, one treatment

Most devices treat skin or muscle. EMFACE treats both at the same time.

  1. Radiofrequency warms the skin to boost collagen and elastin.
  2. Muscle stimulation tones the small muscles that hold your face up.
  3. Together they lift and firm, with nothing that breaks the skin.

FAQ

Good to know

Does it hurt?

Most clients describe warmth and a tapping sensation. No numbing is needed, and the level is adjusted for your comfort.

When will I see results?

Some clients notice a lifted look after the first sessions. Results build over the series and for weeks afterward.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. EMFACE pairs well with Botox, skin boosters and Thermage. Your provider will plan the timing.

Who should not have EMFACE?

It is not recommended during pregnancy or with metal or electronic implants in the face. Your provider will review your history at consultation.
