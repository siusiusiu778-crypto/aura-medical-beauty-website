How it works

Calm first, then glow

Your skin barrier is the outer layer that keeps moisture in and irritants out. When it’s weakened by lasers, microneedling, over-exfoliation or harsh weather, skin can feel tight, sting or turn red. This facial first gently clears the pores, then infuses concentrated repair serums to calm redness, restore hydration and help your skin rebuild. It is the facial we recommend after BBL™, Gold RF Microneedling and Thermage® FLX.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • Sensitive or reactive skin
  • Redness, dryness or stinging
  • Recovering after BBL™, microneedling or Thermage® FLX
  • Skin stressed by seasons, travel or harsh products

Does not treat

  • Stubborn congestion and clogged pores
  • Acne scars or deep texture
  • Pigmentation or sun spots
  • Infections or skin conditions that need medical care

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my skin barrier is damaged?

Common signs are tightness, stinging, redness, flaking, or products that suddenly burn. A Gold Skin Analysis can show us what’s happening.

Can I have it after a laser or microneedling?

Yes, that’s what it’s designed for. After BBL™, Gold RF Microneedling or Thermage® FLX, it helps calm redness and dryness and lowers the risk of post-treatment darkening. While you recover, every 7–10 days works well.

I have rosacea or eczema. Is it right for me?

It’s designed for sensitive skin and can help calm it, but it doesn’t replace medical care. Tell us about your skin history and we’ll plan around it.

What should I know before and after?

Before: For 1 week, avoid sunburn, saunas, waxing, lasers and peels. For 3 days, pause acids, retinol, high-strength vitamin C and scrubs. Reschedule if you have an open wound, cold sore, acute allergy or widespread breakout. Tell us if you’re pregnant or taking medication.

After: Day 1: lukewarm, gentle cleansing and hydration only. Hands off your face. Days 2–3: keep pausing actives and exfoliants. Strict sun protection. Days 4–5: ease back into your usual routine.

When will I see results?

Redness calms and skin looks brighter and smoother right away. Regular treatments help sensitive skin stay stable. Results vary by individual. Every 2–4 weeks for sensitive or fragile skin. Every 7–10 days while recovering after a treatment. Monthly for maintenance.
