FAQs

Membership questions

What does it cost?

AURA Prime is $299 per month, with a 3-month minimum.

How do I join?

Sign up at the front desk, or book a visit online and we’ll set you up.

When does the quarterly BBL™ or Rejuran® unlock?

After 3 consecutive months of membership, and every 3 months after that.

What if I don’t use my facial this month?

Your monthly facial needs to be used within that month and doesn’t carry over.

Do Wellness Credits expire?

No. Credits keep adding up. 500 credits can be redeemed for 1 NAD+ IV Drip.

How do I cancel?

After the 3-month minimum, give us 30 days’ notice.

Who is AURA Prime for?

Anyone who wants consistent, never-overdone results: a facial every month, a seasonal BBL™ or Rejuran® treatment every quarter, and Wellness Credits toward NAD+.

Membership terms
  • AURA Prime has a 3-month minimum.
  • The quarterly BBL™ / Rejuran® perk unlocks after 3 consecutive months.
  • Your monthly facial must be used within that month.
  • Perks not yet unlocked cannot be exchanged for cash or refunded.
  • Wellness Credits keep adding up.
  • To cancel, please give 30 days’ notice.
