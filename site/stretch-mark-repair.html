How it works

Break down, then rebuild

Stretch marks are not a surface problem. When skin stretches quickly, during pregnancy, growth spurts or weight change, the collagen and elastic fibers deep in the skin tear and heal as thin, scar-like lines. Creams can’t reach that depth. Gold RF Microneedling first breaks up the stiff, scarred fibers and opens tiny channels in the skin. Sculptra® then stimulates new collagen to fill in and rebuild the damaged layer.

Treat the cause, not the surface.

Smoother texture

Indented marks look flatter and more even.

Softer color

Red marks fade, and white marks blend in more.

Thicker skin

New collagen and elastic fibers strengthen the area.

Lasting change

Rebuilds the skin’s structure, not just the surface.

Using Sculptra® for stretch marks is off-label. We’ll explain the benefits and risks at your consultation.

Is it right for you?

Ideal for

Ideal for

  • Red or purple stretch marks (newer marks respond fastest)
  • White or silver stretch marks
  • Marks after pregnancy, growth or weight change
  • Thin, crepey skin over the marks

Does not treat

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding (we’ll plan for after)
  • Active infection or open wounds in the area
  • A history of keloid scarring
  • Isotretinoin in the last 6 months

Your treatment

About the treatment

  1. 01
    Two steps
    Gold RF Microneedling breaks up scarred fibers and tightens the skin. Sculptra® (PLLA) then stimulates new collagen to fill in the damaged layer over the following months.
  2. 02
    Why combine them
    Microneedling alone breaks down old scar tissue but can’t rebuild what is missing. Sculptra® alone rebuilds, but can’t break through dense scarring. Together, they do both.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt? Any side effects?

We apply numbing cream first. Most clients feel warmth and pressure during microneedling and light pricks with Sculptra®.

Redness, swelling and tenderness are common and temporary. Small bumps under the skin can occur with Sculptra®, and massage helps prevent them.

Can old, white stretch marks still improve?

Yes. They are the most stubborn, because the damage is older and the scar tissue is denser, which is why we combine both steps. Improvement is gradual.

Can I do it during pregnancy?

No. We’ll plan your treatment for after pregnancy and breastfeeding.

What should I know before and after?

Before: Avoid sunburn and self-tanner on the area. If you’ve taken isotretinoin, please wait 6 months after your last dose. Tell us about any history of keloid scarring.

After: Redness and mild swelling are normal and fade over 3–7 days. Keep the area clean and moisturized, and avoid tight clothing on it for a few days. Massage the area as we show you, to help Sculptra® settle evenly. Skip saunas, hot baths and intense workouts for 7 days.

When will I see results?

Texture and color improve gradually as new collagen forms over the following months. Newer, red marks usually respond fastest. Older, white marks improve more slowly. Results vary by individual. Planned as a series. Your provider will recommend how many sessions you need.
