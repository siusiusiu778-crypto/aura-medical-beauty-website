Myths & Facts

Let’s clear that up

Myth

Expensive creams fix wrinkles.

Fact: Most skincare works on the surface. Deeper changes need treatments that reach the layers below.

Myth

Your skin needs to breathe.

Fact: Skin doesn’t breathe air. It needs a healthy barrier, hydration and daily SPF.

Myth

Filler makes everyone look puffy.

Fact: The right product, in the right layer and amount, looks natural. Planning matters most.
